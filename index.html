Personal project · Live web app · 2026

hikepace: a free hiking planner

Before building anything, I surveyed 14 hikers. 13 of them had packed the wrong amount of food or water. So I built a free web app that estimates time on the trail, daylight, the weather at the top, and how much water and food to bring. It works offline.

I tested it on my own hikes and fixed what it got wrong. For example, it first assumed my pace on a short climb would hold on a 16-mile climb. Now it shows a time range with fatigue and altitude built in, and those ranges covered my real times. I'm now collecting real hike recordings from other hikers to make it more accurate.
